VC Funding Map: AI Agents, NeuroTech, AI Bio & Digital Health

50 representative VC-backed companies across four adjacent sectors — with stage, lead investors, product, and how they actually make money.

By Tzu-Wei Lee · 2026

Where is venture money flowing in the AI-agent and AI-x-bio space, and how do these companies actually earn revenue? This page is a working scan, organised into four adjacent tracks: agent infrastructure, brain-computer interfaces, AI-driven drug discovery, and digital health platforms.

For each company I list founding year, most recent funding round, lead investors, the product, and the revenue mechanism. The list is not exhaustive — it focuses on companies whose business model is informative for the sector as a whole.

Track 1Agent Evaluation & Automation

The infrastructure & trust layer for LLM applications — the “observability and CI” of the agent era.

  • 1. LangChain (LangSmith) smith.langchain.com

    Founded 2022 · Series B+ · Sequoia

    Product. Open-source Python/JS framework for chaining LLM calls, tool use, and retrieval into agents. The hosted layer, LangSmith, captures every prompt, tool call, and intermediate step so developers can debug, evaluate, and monitor agents in production.

    Model. Open-source framework is free (the funnel). LangSmith charges a per-seat developer fee plus a usage fee tied to the number of traces ingested, with an enterprise tier for SOC2 / VPC deployments.

  • 2. DeepEval (Confident AI) confident-ai.com

    Founded 2023 · Seed · YC

    Product. Open-source pytest-style unit-test framework for LLM and agent outputs — hallucination, factual-consistency, toxicity, custom rubric metrics. The Confident AI dashboard layers experiment tracking, regression detection, and CI/CD hooks on top.

    Model. Classic open-core: framework is MIT-licensed; teams pay for the SaaS dashboard, scheduled regression suites, and CI integrations on a per-seat + per-eval basis.

  • 3. Galileo rungalileo.io

    Founded 2023 · Series A · Battery Ventures

    Product. Enterprise-grade GenAI evaluation platform centred on hallucination detection. Proprietary “ChainPoll” and “Luna” metrics flag unsupported claims, prompt drift, and PII leakage across both pre-production evals and live production monitoring.

    Model. Pure enterprise B2B SaaS. Annual platform licence sized by traffic volume and number of models monitored, often six- to seven-figure ACVs for Fortune 500 buyers.

  • 4. Braintrust braintrustdata.com

    Founded 2023 · Series A · a16z, Greylock

    Product. End-to-end workspace for prompt engineering and agent evaluation: a playground for prompt iteration, an experiments view for A/B-testing model versions, a dataset manager, and shared rubrics teams can run in CI.

    Model. B2B SaaS priced by collaboration seats plus compute usage (eval runs and stored traces). Targets engineering teams that have moved past prototypes and need shared infrastructure.

  • 5. Weights & Biases (Weave) wandb.ai

    Founded 2017 · Acquired by CoreWeave (2025, ~$1.7B) · Insight, Sequoia, Coatue

    Product. The de-facto ML experiment tracker (W&B Models) for training runs and hyperparameter sweeps, expanded with W&B Weave for tracing, evaluating, and monitoring LLM and agent applications in production.

    Model. Free tier for individuals and academic users; per-seat team subscriptions; six- to seven-figure enterprise contracts. Now positioned as the eval / observability layer of the CoreWeave AI cloud stack.

  • 6. Arize AI (Phoenix) arize.com

    Founded 2020 · Series B+ · Battery Ventures, Foundation Capital

    Product. ML and LLM observability platform. Phoenix is the open-source tracing and evaluation library (OTel-compatible); Arize AX is the enterprise platform adding drift detection, retrieval debugging, and span-level metrics for agentic systems.

    Model. Classic open-core funnel: Phoenix is free; Arize AX is sold to ML-platform teams at large enterprises on annual contracts sized by data volume and number of models monitored.

  • 7. Humanloop humanloop.com

    Founded 2020 · Series A · Index Ventures

    Product. LLM product-engineering platform: collaborative prompt management with version control, datasets + evaluation pipelines, and human-feedback loops. Used inside Duolingo, Vanta, and other product teams shipping LLM features.

    Model. Per-seat SaaS with usage-based add-ons for eval runs. Sells primarily to product engineering teams (not infra teams), which differentiates it from W&B / Arize.

Track 2Neurotechnology & Brain-Computer Interfaces

The neuro-frontier — ranging from fully invasive implants to non-invasive wearables.

  • 1. Neuralink neuralink.com

    Founded 2016 · Series D+ · Founders Fund

    Product. High-channel-count invasive BCI: the N1 implant has >1,000 electrodes on flexible threads, placed by the purpose-built R1 surgical robot. Initial clinical indication is restoring digital-device control for people with severe motor impairment (ALS, spinal-cord injury).

    Model. Pre-revenue, R&D-stage. Eventual model will follow the high-cost medical-device path — per-implant device sale, surgical service fees, and Medicare/private-insurance reimbursement once an FDA indication is granted.

  • 2. Synchron synchron.com

    Founded 2016 · Series C+ · Khosla, ARCH

    Product. Stentrode — a stent-mounted electrode array delivered through the jugular vein into the brain’s motor cortex, removing the need for open craniotomy. Lower channel count than Neuralink, but installable by existing endovascular surgeons.

    Model. Standard high-end medical-device pathway: FDA clearance, then hospital sales backed by CMS / private-payer reimbursement. The minimally-invasive procedure is positioned as a faster, broader market path than craniotomy-based BCIs.

  • 3. Kernel kernel.com

    Founded 2016 · Series C · General Catalyst

    Product. Kernel Flow — a non-invasive, wearable time-domain functional near-infrared spectroscopy (TD-fNIRS) helmet that measures cortical haemoglobin response at MRI-comparable spatial resolution, without a magnet.

    Model. Hardware sale plus “brain data as a service”: research labs and pharma buy or rent helmets, plus paid access to standardised datasets collected across the deployed fleet.

  • 4. Rune Labs runelabs.io

    Founded 2018 · Series A+ · Eclipse Ventures

    Product. StrivePD platform — ingests data from Apple Watch, deep-brain-stimulation devices (Medtronic Percept), and patient-reported outcomes to give clinicians and pharma a longitudinal view of Parkinson’s symptoms outside the clinic.

    Model. B2B platform subscription. Pharma pays for de-identified objective endpoints in clinical trials; device makers pay for post-market surveillance; health systems pay for population dashboards.

  • 5. Paradromics paradromics.com

    Founded 2015 · Series A+ · Prime Movers Lab

    Product. Connexus Direct Data Interface — one of the highest-density microelectrode arrays in development, with thousands of channels aimed at high-bandwidth speech and motor decoding.

    Model. Medical-device hardware sales once cleared; near-term revenue from research collaborations and government grants while in clinical development.

  • 6. Precision Neuroscience precisionneuro.io

    Founded 2021 · Series C · Forepont, B Capital

    Product. Layer 7 Cortical Interface — a flexible, paper-thin micro-electrode array placed on the cortical surface through a thin cranial slot, without penetrating brain tissue. Holds an FDA Breakthrough Device designation and has been used intraoperatively in dozens of patients to date.

    Model. Standard high-end medical-device pathway — FDA clearance, hospital sales, and reimbursement. Founding team includes ex-Neuralink co-founder Ben Rapoport, and the company positions itself as a less-risky surgical alternative to penetrating-electrode BCIs.

Track 3AI Drug Discovery & Bio Infrastructure

AI × bio heavyweights — from cell-programming foundries to physics-based simulation to in-house pipelines.

  • 1. Ginkgo Bioworks ginkgobioworks.com

    Founded 2008 · Public (NYSE: DNA) · YC (early)

    Product. Foundry — a highly automated facility for engineering custom microorganisms (yeast, bacteria) for pharma, agriculture, fragrance, and materials. Customers describe an organism’s desired function; Ginkgo iterates strains.

    Model. Two-stream: upfront foundry “R&D services” fees per programme plus downstream value — royalties, milestones, or equity in the customer’s commercial product.

  • 2. Recursion recursion.com

    Founded 2013 · Public (Nasdaq: RXRX) · Baillie Gifford

    Product. An “operating system for biology” built on petabytes of standardised cellular images. Machine-learning models map gene perturbations and compound exposures to morphological phenotypes, identifying drug candidates for rare disease, oncology, and inflammation.

    Model. Hybrid: long-term pharma partnerships (Bayer, Roche-Genentech) that pay platform-access fees and milestones, plus a wholly-owned clinical pipeline whose upside accrues to Recursion if approved.

  • 3. Schrödinger schrodinger.com

    Founded 1990 · Public (Nasdaq: SDGR) · Gates Foundation

    Product. Physics-based molecular modelling suite (Maestro, FEP+, Glide) used across most large pharma for protein-ligand binding-affinity prediction, free-energy calculations, and lead optimisation. Decades-deep validation data.

    Model. Two parallel businesses: high-margin enterprise software subscriptions and an internal therapeutics pipeline licensed out to large pharma at high-value milestone terms.

  • 4. Genesis Therapeutics genesistherapeutics.ai

    Founded 2019 · Series B · a16z

    Product. GEMS platform — deep-learning molecular generation tuned for small molecules in “difficult” chemistry: undruggable targets, allosteric pockets, and selectivity-constrained scaffolds. Generates synthetically accessible candidates rather than virtual compounds.

    Model. Co-development deals where Genesis is the AI-design half (upfront + milestones + royalties), alongside a wholly-owned pipeline that captures full upside on chosen targets.

  • 5. BigHat Biosciences bighatbio.com

    Founded 2019 · Series B · a16z

    Product. Milliner™ platform — tightly coupled ML + high-throughput antibody wet lab. Generates diverse antibody candidates against multi-objective property profiles (affinity, viscosity, half-life) and iterates in days rather than months.

    Model. Large-pharma co-development partnerships (Amgen, Merck) for specific therapeutic programmes, with upfront, success-based milestones, and tiered royalties.

  • 6. Asimov asimov.com

    Founded 2017 · Series B · a16z

    Product. Kernel — a CAD-style platform for designing mammalian-cell genetic circuits, especially for biologics manufacturing (CHO cells producing antibodies). Pairs design software with characterised “genetic part” libraries the customer can compose.

    Model. Software licence to biotech and pharma customers, bundled with proprietary consumables (engineered cell lines, validated genetic parts) sold per programme.

  • 7. Scribe Therapeutics scribetx.com

    Founded 2017 · Series B · a16z

    Product. CRISPR by Design™ — engineered CasX enzymes that are smaller, more specific, and more deliverable than wild-type Cas9, aimed at in vivo therapeutic editing.

    Model. Platform licensing: large pharma (e.g. Sanofi, Prevail) pay upfront, programme fees, and milestone-based royalties to use Scribe enzymes inside their pipelines, while Scribe also advances internal targets.

  • 8. BioAge Labs bioagelabs.com

    Founded 2015 · Series D · a16z

    Product. Discovery engine that mines human longitudinal biobanks (proteomics, transcriptomics linked to lifespan/healthspan outcomes) to identify molecular drivers of healthy ageing — especially around muscle mass and metabolic health.

    Model. “Asset-driven”: in-licence existing shelved compounds cheaply, validate them in an ageing indication, and out-licence late-stage assets to large pharma at high upfront + royalties.

  • 9. Valo Health valohealth.com

    Founded 2019 · Series C · a16z

    Product. Opal — an end-to-end drug-discovery platform built on human multi-omics datasets, designed to keep target identification, generative chemistry, ADMET, and translational data in a single loop rather than across stitched vendors.

    Model. Combines platform-access partnerships with large pharma and a wholly-owned pipeline; revenue from upfront/milestones near term, future revenue from owned assets reaching late-stage trials.

  • 10. Camp4 Therapeutics camp4tx.com

    Founded 2016 · Series B · a16z

    Product. RAP platform — targets regulatory RNAs (eRNAs / lncRNAs) to dial up the expression of disease-relevant genes, opening a new modality alongside silencing-style RNA drugs.

    Model. Target-discovery collaborations with pharma (upfront + per-target milestones) plus internal programmes in genetic liver disease and CNS indications.

  • 11. Atomwise atomwise.com

    Founded 2012 · Series B+ · YC, Tencent

    Product. AtomNet — an early production-deployed CNN for structure-based virtual screening. Customers point it at a target pocket; AtomNet ranks millions of commercially-available molecules in days at fraction of wet-lab cost.

    Model. Two streams: project-based research collaborations with pharma and academic groups, plus joint-venture programmes where Atomwise retains long-term economics on resulting assets.

  • 12. Culture Biosciences culturebiosciences.com

    Founded 2016 · Series B · YC, a16z

    Product. Cloud Bioreactors — remotely controlled 250 mL and 1 L bioreactors operated in Culture’s own facility. Customers design experiments in a web app; Culture runs and streams real-time data back. Removes the need to staff an in-house fermentation lab.

    Model. “Lab as a service” — usage-based pricing per reactor-hour or per experiment, plus consumables. Targets fast-moving startups and biopharma teams that need to iterate strain conditions weekly.

  • 13. Notable Labs notablelabs.com

    Founded 2014 · Series B · YC

    Product. Predictive Precision Medicine Platform — takes a patient’s leukaemia or lymphoma sample, exposes it ex vivo to a panel of drugs, and combines flow-cytometry plus ML to predict clinical response before treatment.

    Model. Combines reference-laboratory fees per assay with a wholly-owned drug pipeline (e.g. fosciclopirox in AML) advanced with risk-shared trial designs that pair patient enrolment to predicted responders.

  • 14. Owkin owkin.com

    Founded 2016 · Series C (Unicorn) · Sanofi, Google

    Product. Federated-learning network that trains AI models across hospital-held multi-omics and histopathology data without that data ever leaving each hospital. Used to discover biomarkers and stratify patients in oncology.

    Model. Strategic partnerships with large pharma (notably Sanofi, $180M deal) covering upfront fees, target-based milestones, and royalty participation on assets co-discovered with the platform.

  • 15. Tempus tempus.com

    Founded 2015 · Public (Nasdaq: TEM) · Baillie Gifford

    Product. Library of de-identified clinical + molecular records spanning oncology, cardiology, and depression, sourced from genomic-sequencing services Tempus runs for partner hospitals.

    Model. Two reinforcing legs: per-sample sequencing/diagnostic fees billed to hospitals/payers (Genomics business), and large-deal data licences to pharma (Data & Services business) where most of the long-term value sits.

  • 16. Isomorphic Labs isomorphiclabs.com

    Founded 2021 · ~$600M first external round (2024) · Thrive Capital, Alphabet

    Product. Spin-out of Alphabet’s DeepMind built around AlphaFold and its successors. Combines protein-structure prediction with generative chemistry to enumerate candidate drugs in silico, then validates them with partner wet labs.

    Model. Large-pharma research deals — multi-target collaborations with Novartis (~$1.2B) and Eli Lilly (~$1.7B) in 2024 covering upfront payments, per-target milestones, and royalties — plus a wholly-owned pipeline in oncology and immunology.

  • 17. Insilico Medicine insilico.com

    Founded 2014 · Series E · Sanofi, WuXi, Warburg Pincus

    Product. Pharma.AI — integrated platform spanning PandaOmics (target discovery from multi-omics), Chemistry42 (generative chemistry), and InClinico (trial-outcome prediction). Lead asset INS018_055 for idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis is one of the first fully AI-discovered drugs in Phase II.

    Model. Three streams: SaaS licences of Pharma.AI modules to pharma; multi-target collaborations (notably a $1.2B Sanofi deal in 2022); and an owned clinical pipeline whose late-stage assets are candidates for out-licensing.

Track 4Digital Health & Data Platforms

Tech-enabled payers, providers, diagnostics, and software — companies operating inside the existing healthcare reimbursement system.

  • 1. Freenome freenome.com

    Founded 2014 · Series E · a16z, Roche

    Product. Multi-omics blood test combining cell-free DNA, methylation, and protein signals with ML to detect early-stage cancer — lead programme is colorectal cancer screening as an alternative to colonoscopy/FIT.

    Model. Reimbursed per-test pricing once on national guidelines (Medicare + commercial payers), plus companion-diagnostic revenue from pharma clinical trials in the meantime.

  • 2. Flatiron Health flatiron.com

    Founded 2012 · Acquired by Roche (2018, $1.9B) · Google Ventures, ICONIQ

    Product. OncoEMR — an oncology-specialty electronic medical record used at hundreds of community cancer centres — plus a curated real-world oncology dataset built from that EMR footprint.

    Model. Hospital SaaS for OncoEMR and OncoBilling; data licences to pharma for clinical-trial design and regulatory submissions (RWD endpoints). Operates as a Roche subsidiary post-acquisition.

  • 3. TrialSpark trialspark.com

    Founded 2013 · Series C · Thrive, Sequoia

    Product. Originally a software-driven CRO running trials inside primary-care clinics with proprietary trial-recruitment infrastructure. Has since pivoted into Formation Bio, an in-licensing biotech that uses its trial machinery on drugs it owns.

    Model. Shifted from CRO fee-for-service to drug-development economics — buy promising assets from biotech cheaply, run them more efficiently, capture full upside on approval.

  • 4. Devoted Health devoted.com

    Founded 2017 · Series E · a16z

    Product. Tech-enabled Medicare Advantage health plan for US seniors. Owns the full stack — software for member engagement, an in-house clinical team for high-cost members, and the insurance entity itself.

    Model. Capitated payments from the federal government per enrolled senior. Profit comes from the spread between CMS payments and actual medical costs, which Devoted aims to lower via proactive primary-care interventions.

  • 5. Omada Health omadahealth.com

    Founded 2011 · Series E · a16z

    Product. Digital-therapeutic programmes for diabetes prevention, diabetes management, hypertension, and musculoskeletal pain. Combines an app, a connected scale or BP cuff, and human health coaches.

    Model. Per-member-per-month subscriptions sold to self-insured employers and health plans, often with a pay-for-performance component — fees tied to weight loss or A1C reduction milestones.

  • 6. Komodo Health komodohealth.com

    Founded 2014 · Series E · a16z, ICONIQ

    Product. “Healthcare Map” — a longitudinal dataset linking ~330M US patient journeys across diagnoses, prescriptions, and provider visits, queried via Sentinel and MapEnhance applications.

    Model. Multi-year enterprise SaaS contracts with pharma (commercial-strategy use), payers (network-design use), and providers. Typical deal sizes in the high six to low seven figures.

  • 7. PatientPing (now Bamboo Health) bamboohealth.com

    Founded 2013 · Acquired by Appriss (2021) · a16z

    Product. Real-time care-coordination network — pings clinicians the moment a shared patient hits an ED, is admitted, or is discharged anywhere on the network. Reduces readmissions and improves post-acute follow-up.

    Model. SaaS subscriptions to hospitals, ACOs, payers, and post-acute facilities, sized by lives covered. Now sold as part of Bamboo Health’s broader care-coordination suite.

  • 8. Athelas athelas.com

    Founded 2016 · Series B · YC

    Product. At-home connected diagnostic devices (white-blood-cell counter, blood-pressure cuff, glucometer) that stream vitals to a clinical operations team. Originally aimed at clozapine-monitored patients; expanded to chronic-care RPM and revenue-cycle software.

    Model. Hardware bundled essentially free with services. Revenue from CPT-coded Remote Patient Monitoring (RPM) and Chronic Care Management (CCM) reimbursement billed to Medicare and commercial payers each month.

  • 9. BioRender biorender.com

    Founded 2017 · Series A · YC

    Product. The “Canva for life sciences” — a drag-and-drop illustration tool with thousands of scientifically-accurate icons (organs, cells, molecules) used to make figures for papers, posters, and grants.

    Model. Product-led freemium SaaS. Individuals use it free with watermarks; lab teams and biotech companies pay per-seat annual subscriptions for high-resolution export, custom assets, and team libraries.

  • 10. Volumetric (now 3D Systems Bioprinting) 3dsystems.com/bioprinting

    Founded 2018 · Acquired by 3D Systems (2021) · YC

    Product. Stereolithography-based bioprinters capable of resolving fine vasculature inside printed tissue constructs — a step toward printable, perfusable organs.

    Model. Capital-equipment sale of the printer plus razor-blade recurring revenue from proprietary photoreactive bio-inks and cartridges.

  • 11. Miroculus (now part of Integra Biosciences) miroculus.com

    Founded 2014 · Acquired by Integra Biosciences (2024) · YC

    Product. Miro Canvas — benchtop digital microfluidics system that automates NGS library prep and other multi-step molecular protocols on a single chip, with minimal user setup.

    Model. Razor-and-blades — the instrument is the razor, the single-use proprietary cartridges and reagent kits are the recurring blade revenue per protocol run.

  • 12. Nurx (Thirty Madison) nurx.com

    Founded 2015 · Merged with Thirty Madison (2022) · YC

    Product. Telehealth direct-to-consumer service for women’s health (birth control, emergency contraception, HPV screening, migraine, dermatology). Asynchronous clinical review plus mail-order pharmacy.

    Model. Subscription prescription fulfilment with long-tail per-month drug margin (especially contraceptives), supplemented by per-consultation fees and insurance billing where applicable.

  • 13. Curative curative.com

    Founded 2020 · Private · YC

    Product. Originated as a COVID-19 PCR testing platform optimised for logistics and turnaround. Has since pivoted into employer health-insurance plans built around proactive primary-care “baseline visits.”

    Model. Originally fee-for-service reimbursement per COVID test (federal payer). Now monthly per-member-per-month employer premium revenue, similar to Devoted but for the employer market.

  • 14. Oscar Health hioscar.com

    Founded 2012 · Public (NYSE: OSCR) · Thrive

    Product. Tech-driven health-insurance plans on the ACA individual marketplace, plus a +Oscar enterprise software platform sold to other insurers wanting to modernise their member experience.

    Model. Premium revenue from policy holders. Profit margin depends on the gap between premium intake and medical-loss ratio (claim payouts), which software is meant to compress over time.

  • 15. Cedar cedar.com

    Founded 2016 · Series D · Thrive, a16z

    Product. Patient billing and payment platform layered over hospital revenue-cycle systems. Sends plain-language, mobile-first bills, surfaces price estimates, and offers no-fee payment plans.

    Model. Combination of annual platform SaaS fees to hospitals and a transaction fee on every patient payment that flows through Cedar — the larger health-system contracts compound both legs.

  • 16. Aetion aetion.com

    Founded 2012 · Series C · Thrive

    Product. Aetion Evidence Platform — transparent, reproducible analytics on real-world data (claims, EMRs, registries) so pharma and FDA reviewers can answer comparative-effectiveness and safety questions without bespoke statistician work.

    Model. Enterprise SaaS subscriptions to pharma (regulatory and HEOR teams) and government regulators, sometimes augmented by analytic-services engagements.

  • 17. PathAI pathai.com

    Founded 2016 · Series C+ · General Catalyst

    Product. AI-augmented digital pathology — deep-learning models for tumour grading, biomarker quantification (PD-L1, HER2), and trial-eligibility screening on whole-slide images. Includes a contract-research lab arm (acquired Poplar).

    Model. Two streams: pharma contracts for clinical-trial enrollment and companion-diagnostic development (large milestone-based deals), and a SaaS pathology platform sold to hospital labs.

  • 18. Spring Health springhealth.com

    Founded 2016 · Series D · Tiger Global

    Product. Employer-benefits platform that triages each employee’s self-reported symptoms with an ML model and matches them to the modality most likely to work for them (self-guided content, coaching, therapy, psychiatry, or specialist care).

    Model. Per-employee-per-month subscription sold to large employers, with utilisation-based fees stacked on top for actual therapy hours, psychiatry visits, and specialised programmes.

  • 19. Hinge Health hingehealth.com

    Founded 2014 · Public (NYSE: HNGE, May 2025) · Coatue, Tiger Global

    Product. Digital programmes for musculoskeletal (MSK) pain, women’s pelvic health, and acute injury. Pairs wearable motion sensors and an in-app exercise library with remote physical therapists and health coaches — one of the largest digital-therapeutics deployments worldwide.

    Model. Per-employee-per-month subscriptions sold to large self-insured employers and health plans, often with shared-savings or pay-for-performance components linked to reductions in MSK claims spend.

  • 20. Lyra Health lyrahealth.com

    Founded 2015 · Series F · Coatue, Sands Capital

    Product. Mental-health benefits platform for large employers (Morgan Stanley, Amgen, Starbucks, etc.). Combines ML-driven care navigation with a curated network of therapists trained in evidence-based modalities (CBT, ACT), plus coaching, psychiatry, and specialised programmes for complex care.

    Model. Per-employee-per-month enterprise subscription plus utilisation-based fees per therapy session, psychiatry visit, or specialised programme — the closer enterprise-tier competitor to Spring Health.
