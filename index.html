  2. The First Mouse Spinal Cord fMRI (under review)
    T.-W. Lee.
    2025. Manuscript draft page

    Since the 1990s, scientists have mapped functional networks in the spinal cord — showing that, like the brain, its regions continuously “talk” to each other. These networks are now well established in humans and even non-human primates. But why haven’t we reliably studied them in mice? We actually could — but what are the limits?

    • #Physics
    • #Hardware
    • #Design
    • #Statistics
    • #Neuroscience
  3. Brain Study — Linking Neural Signal to fMRI Power Spectrum
    T.-W. Lee.
    2025. Manuscript draft page

    We mostly understand the brain through the cortex. But what if our interpretation of brain signals is oversimplified — focusing only on the temporal waves in noticeable regions? By treating these signals as dynamic systems and simulating different scenarios across regions, we can uncover hidden cause-and-effect relationships.
