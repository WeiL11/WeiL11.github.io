About

Hello! I'm Charles (Tzu-Wei, 李梓維), a Ph.D. candidate in Biomedical Engineering at Vanderbilt University, advised by Prof. John C. Gore at VUIIS. I bring 8+ years in biotechnology and neuroscience and 7+ years of machine learning research.

My work spans MRI physics — pulse sequence programming and image reconstruction at 3T, 7T, and 15.2T — multimodal representation learning on large-scale neuroimaging and genomics data, and LLM agent systems for clinical decision support. On the side, I dig into efficient LLM inference (vLLM, SGLang, Triton).
